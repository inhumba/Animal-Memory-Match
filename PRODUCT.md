# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Early elementary children (ages 6–8) playing independently on phones, tablets, or desktop browsers. They are building memory skills and learning object/animal names through repeated matches. Parents or caregivers may launch the game and supervise, but the child is the primary player.

## Product Purpose

Animal Memory Match is a single-file, zero-install memory game that turns card matching into light vocabulary practice. Speech synthesis pronounces each item when flipped; procedural audio and canvas animations reward matches without external assets. Success means a child can pick a theme, choose difficulty, complete a round, and feel proud—without adult help reading instructions.

## Brand Personality

Playful, warm, encouraging. The tone celebrates effort ("Amazing Job!") rather than competition. UI copy is short, friendly, and direct. Visual energy comes from emoji, color, and motion—not from corporate edtech chrome.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI kids app" aesthetics: soft pastel gradient backgrounds, identical bubbly card grids, glassmorphism headers, and rainbow gradient CTAs stacked on every screen.
- Corporate edtech dashboards with badges, streaks, and "learning platform" framing.
- Overstimulating sensory overload: too many simultaneous animations, sounds, and competing accent colors.

## Design Principles

1. **Celebrate effort, not competition.** Feedback should feel like encouragement from a patient teacher, not a scoreboard battle. Victories are joyful; mismatches are gentle.
2. **Learning through play.** Speech and visuals turn each flip into a micro-lesson. Features earn their place when they help a child remember a name or pattern—not when they add chrome.
3. **Big targets, clear states.** Touch areas stay large; during play the header shows flips and pairs left (muted audio state is always reachable). Score and best results appear after a round — not mid-game — so progress stays readable without pressure. A 6-year-old should never wonder what to tap next.
4. **Delight with restraint.** Animation, particles, and sound are rewards tied to actions—not ambient noise. One strong moment beats five competing effects.
5. **Premium through craft, not clutter.** A polished v1 comes from sharp canvas rendering, responsive layout, and cohesive typography—not from adding more UI layers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basic inclusive defaults: large touch targets, readable text at mobile sizes, and clear visual feedback on interaction. Mute control covers both speech and SFX. No formal WCAG certification target yet; future passes should verify contrast on stat pills and setup buttons.
